Find answers to common questions about ExcuseGen Pro

How does the AI generate believable excuses?

Our AI analyzes thousands of real-world excuse scenarios and professional communications to understand what makes an excuse believable. It considers context, tone, and common professional scenarios to generate excuses that sound natural and appropriate for your specific situation.

Is my data secure and private?

Yes, we take privacy seriously. Your inputs and generated excuses are never stored on our servers or shared with third parties. We use encryption for all data transmission and follow strict data protection protocols.

Can I use ExcuseGen Pro for academic purposes?

Absolutely. Our tool is designed to help in various professional and academic contexts. However, we encourage responsible use and remind users that academic integrity should always be maintained.

What's the difference between free and paid plans?

The free plan allows for 5 excuses per day with basic customization options. The Pro plan offers unlimited excuses, advanced customization, priority support, and export functionality. The Team plan adds multiple user accounts and usage analytics.
